About

All questions
Do I have to book right away? Do slots expire?

No, and no. A slot holds its value until you use it, and you choose the date: before a release, after a big game, ahead of a show.

Can I mix Poly and Glass?

Yes. Choose Glass or Poly per card, in the same order: add each housing to your cart and they check out together. Up to 100 per housing per order, and you can place as many orders as you like.

What can I send?

Any sports card or TCG card. Presale slots cover standard size cards; booklets, folds and decks are booked separately. HGA Poly has 60+ sizes tooled to the card, and if no case exists for your card yet, we make one at no extra fee.

Can I transfer or gift a slot?

No. Pre-launch purchases are tied to the purchasing account and cannot be transferred.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final, and pre-launch purchases cannot be transferred.
