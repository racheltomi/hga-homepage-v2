Our process

Five departments. Fourteen steps. Nothing skips ahead.

Each step writes one event to your card's record, and your portal reads that record. You see what happened, when, and the photo, video or images that go with it.

01

Intake & Imaging

  1. 0

    Order placed

    List each card and pick its housing. Pay, then ship. Your tracking link sits in the portal.

  2. 1

    Received & unboxed

    Your box is scanned and photographed on arrival. Opened on camera against your list, every card barcoded.

  3. 2

    Imaged

    High resolution scans of the front and back. Every later step uses these images.

02

ID & Authentication

  1. 3

    Identified

    Matched to the database and confirmed by a person.

  2. 4

    Authenticated

    Measured, UV, magnified, signature checked. It must clear every test. The case size that fits is selected.

03

Grading

  1. 5

    Centering measured

    Borders measured on both sides. The published scale sets the centering grade.

  2. 6

    Graded blind

    Every defect marked on the images by a grader who never knows whose card it is.

  3. 7

    Audited & released

    A senior grader inspects blind and releases the grade. Cert assigned. Grade hidden until the Reveal.

04

Encapsulation

  1. 8

    Sealed

    Label printed, card sealed in the housing you chose. The NFT chip in the label is written to the record.

  2. 9

    Quality checked

    Five checks on the sealed slab. A fail goes back to encapsulation.

  3. 10

    Slab imaged

    The finished slab, front and back, grade masked.

05

Fulfillment

  1. 11

    Ready

    Every card in your submission is in. A second person counts it. You are notified.

  2. 12

    The Reveal

    Turn each slab over live. Then decide, per card: ship home, list, or vault. 7 days.

  3. 13

    Dispatched

    Ship home cards are packed and tracked. List and vault cards stay with us, in your record.

Grade hidden from step 6 until the Reveal, so every customer sees it the same way.
