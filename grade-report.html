How it is graded

Graded blind.
To a published standard.

The same standard for every card, published in full.

Graded blind

Centering set by the published scale. Graded by a grader who never knows whose card it is, then audited for consistency.

Authenticated first

Every card is authenticated before it is graded. A card that cannot be authenticated is returned ungraded, fee refunded.

Verify before you buy

A matching cert number does not by itself prove a slab. Tap the NFT chip in the label to open the record, compare the card in front of you with the images, and buy from sellers you trust.
