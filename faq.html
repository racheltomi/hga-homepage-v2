Presale and slots

What exactly am I buying?

A slot: prepaid grading for one card, in the housing you choose, back within 30 days of your grading date. Every subgrade, the Grade Report and the case are included. Your slots live on your account.

What is the HGA Grading Calendar?

It's how you book your grade. Pick your grading date, and we reserve grading capacity for it before your card ships. We plan capacity up front instead of guessing at a turnaround, so every presale grade is back within 30 days of your grading date. The calendar opens January 18, 2027: we email you an invite to book, and your presale credit applies automatically. Grading begins February 1, 2027.

When does the presale run?

From November 23, 2026 to January 31, 2027. The presale price ends when the presale does, and it does not come back.

How do I use a slot?

From January 18 the grading calendar opens. Book your grading date, assign one slot per card, and ship your cards any time before your date. Each day has a capacity; a full day grays out.

Do I have to book right away? Do slots expire?

No, and no. A slot holds its value until you use it, and you choose the date: before a product release, after a big game, ahead of a show, whenever suits you.

How many slots can I buy?

Up to 100 per housing per order, and there is no limit on the number of orders.

Can I mix Poly and Glass in one order?

Yes. Choose Glass or Poly per card, in the same order. Add each housing to your cart; they check out together.

Can I transfer or gift a pre-launch slot?

No. Pre-launch purchases are tied to the purchasing account and cannot be transferred.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final, and pre-launch purchases cannot be transferred.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Stripe card payments, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Sales tax is added at checkout from your billing address.

Will presale pricing come back?

No. Presale pricing ends January 31, and February 1 is our full launch. Post-launch pricing will be published Feb 1.

Pricing and fees

What does a card cost to grade?

At presale pricing: $29 a card in HGA Poly, $200 a card in HGA Glass. At launch, the same grading is $150 a card in Poly and $600 a card in Glass.

Are there any extra fees?

For any card worth under $10,000, no. Above $10,000, a 2% insurance and handling upcharge applies. The slot covers grading, all subgrades, the Grade Report, autograph grading, the case, the NFT chip and live tracking. You pay for shipping when you book your date.

What about cards over $10,000?

There is no upcharge under $10,000 in card value. Above that, a 2% insurance and handling upcharge applies, paid when you reserve. The slot still covers the grade.

Who pays for shipping?

You choose the shipping, both directions, and pay for it at checkout when you book your grading date.

What if an earlier date opens up?

Choose "move me up automatically" when you book, and we'll move you to an earlier date if one opens.

Is the autograph graded at extra cost?

No. Every autograph is graded free. A 10 goes on the label; anything less stays in your Grade Report.

What is launch pricing?

At launch, grading is $150 a card in HGA Poly and $600 a card in HGA Glass. Presale slots save up to 80%, and presale pricing ends January 31. Pricing changes after Feb 1, 2027.

What will grading cost after launch?

Post-launch pricing will be published Feb 1. Until January 31, presale slots are $29 a card in Poly and $200 a card in Glass.

Why isn't pricing set by card value?

Because the work is the same whatever your card is worth. You pay for the housing you choose, with no card value upcharge under $10,000.

Do booklets cost more?

Presale slots cover standard size cards. Booklets, folds and decks are booked separately; post-launch pricing will be published Feb 1.

Does my card's value change the grading price?

No. Prices follow the housing, not your card's value. The only value based cost is insurance handling for cards over $10,000.

Grading and standards

Who grades my card?

An HGA grader, blind: the grader never knows whose card it is. Every card is authenticated before it is graded, then graded blind, to HGA's published standard, then audited.

What is the HGA grading scale?

Whole numbers from 1 to 10, plus HGA Flawless above Gem Mint 10, and Authentic for real, unaltered cards that can't be graded. No half points on the card grade.

What are the four subgrades?

Centering, corners, edges and surface. Each is scored on its own and printed on the back of every label.

How is centering measured?

With a digital centering tool on the front and back, confirmed by hand. Centering can cap a card's grade. The full front and back thresholds are on the Grading Standards page.

What does Flawless mean?

A perfect 10 on every subgrade earns HGA Flawless and Glass at no charge.

Do you grade altered cards?

No. Altered or counterfeit cards are never graded or cased. Any sign of trimming, recoloring, repair or cleaning sends the card to our authentication team first. A card that cannot be authenticated is returned ungraded, fee refunded.

Can I appeal a grade?

There is no formal appeal. Support is built into your portal, and you can ask about any grade from the card itself.

Is the full standard public?

Yes, in full, on the Grading Standards page. Every card is graded blind, to that standard, then audited.

Housings and card sizes

What is the difference between Poly and Glass?

Same grading, same report, same 30 days. HGA Poly is a clear case in 60+ sizes tooled to the card, matched automatically. HGA Glass is a metal and glass housing, a display piece for premium cards, not a plastic slab. Choose Glass or Poly per card, in the same order.

What can I send?

Any sports card or TCG card. Presale slots cover standard size cards; booklets, folds and decks are booked separately. HGA Poly has 60+ sizes tooled to the card, and if no case exists for your card yet, we make one at no extra fee. HGA Glass is tooled to every card size; if no exact Glass size fits, the nearest is selected and you are told.

Do you grade booklet cards?

Yes. HGA Poly is tooled for booklets, and unique booklet formats are graded as a single card. Presale slots cover standard size cards, so booklets are booked separately.

My card is an unusual size. Will it fit?

HGA Poly comes in 60+ sizes tooled to the card, matched automatically. If no case exists for your card yet, we make one at no extra fee. If no exact Glass size fits, the nearest is selected and you are told.

What is the chip in the label?

An NFT chip tied to the card's permanent record. Tap the label with your phone and the record opens: grade, subgrades, images and the Grade Report. A swapped label or case won't match its permanent record.

Shipping and tracking

When does my grade come back?

Every presale grade, Poly and Glass, is back within 30 days of your grading date.

Can I see my cards while they're with HGA?

Yes. Your portal tracks every step live: a photo of your package on arrival, a video of us opening it and removing your cards, high resolution images of each card on arrival, and final images of each graded slab.

What if my card is lost or damaged?

If a card is lost or damaged in our care, we replace it or pay it out. Every time. Every step is documented in your portal.

What happens after the Reveal?

You decide what happens to each slab: ship home, list, or vault, within 7 days.

When do I send my cards?

Any time before your booked grading date. Your confirmation page tells you when.

Your grade and report

What is the Grade Report?

The full record behind your grade: every defect a grader found, where it is on the card, how severe, with images. It is public on the cert.

What is the Reveal?

The moment you turn each slab over live in your portal and see the grade, the subgrades and the Grade Report. Then decide: ship home, list, or vault, within 7 days.

How do I verify a slab?

A matching cert number does not by itself prove a slab. Tap the NFT chip in the label to open the record, compare the card in front of you with the images, and buy from sellers you trust.

Is my grade public?

The cert record and the Grade Report are public on the cert, so anyone can check an HGA slab against its record.

Legacy HGA

Can I look up my legacy HGA slab?

Yes. Enter the cert number from your label on the Legacy HGA page.

Can I move a legacy slab into the new housings?

Yes. Legacy HGA slabs can come back for recertification to the new standard and the new housings. Pricing publishes before recertification opens.

Will legacy grades appear in the population report?

Legacy HGA slabs join the report as they are recertified to the current standard.

Can I cross over a slab from another company?

Yes. Other companies' slabs cross over the same way: recertified to the HGA standard and rehoused.

About HGA

Who is behind HGA?

HGA is based in Chandler, Arizona. HGA is owned by uCollect and grades independently. HGA does not buy, sell, or own cards. We grade them.

What does Hybrid Grading Approach mean?

Expert graders working to a published standard, with software recording every finding. People make the calls; the record makes them visible.

What changed at HGA?

HGA had real problems in the past: turnaround times, housings and more. We rebuilt it around a grading date you book, a published standard, new housings and a full report on every card.
