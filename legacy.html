All legacy questions
Can I look up my legacy HGA slab?

Yes. Enter the cert number from your label in the lookup at the top of this page.

Do I have to recertify my legacy slabs?

No. Recertification is optional. It gives the card a grade to the current standard, four subgrades, a full report and a new housing.

What will recertification cost?

Pricing publishes before recertification opens. Leave your email above and we'll send it the day it's live.

Could my card get a different grade?

Yes. Recertified cards are graded blind to today's published standard, so the new grade can differ from the legacy one. The full Grade Report shows exactly why.

Will legacy grades appear in the population report?

Legacy HGA slabs join the report as they are recertified to the current standard.

Can I cross over a slab from another company?

Yes. Other companies' slabs cross over the same way: recertified to the HGA standard and rehoused.
