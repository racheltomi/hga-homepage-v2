FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the new HGA?

It's HGA's rebuilt grading system. Instead of sending your cards into a "black box," you can follow your card from delivery to grade reveal, with card tracking, a grading date you book, a full grading report with subgrades, and visible proof behind your grade. Every card is graded blind, to a published standard, then audited.

What is the HGA Grading Calendar?

It's how you book your grade. Pick your grading date, and we reserve grading capacity for it before your card ships. We plan capacity up front instead of guessing at a turnaround, so every presale grade is back within 30 days of your grading date. The calendar opens January 18, 2027 for you to use your pre-sale slots to book your grading date. Grading begins February 1, 2027.

What's the turnaround?

Every presale grade, Poly and Glass, is back within 30 days of your grading date.

What if an earlier date opens up?

Choose "move me up automatically" when you book, and we'll move you to an earlier date if one opens.

What's the difference between the Poly and Glass slots?

Poly ($29 pre-launch) gets your card professionally graded, protected, and verified. Glass ($200 pre-launch) includes everything in Poly, plus premium metal and glass housing, a museum-worthy card presentation, and a premium display experience for your most prized collectibles.

Is there an upcharge for high-value cards?

There's no card-value upcharge on cards valued under $10,000. For cards valued above $10,000, a 2% insurance and handling upcharge applies.

How does NFT chip verification work?

Tap the NFT chip in your card's label with your phone to open its permanent record. A swapped label or case won't match its permanent record. Each card is also blockchain-certified, adding a layer of protection against card swapping and fraudulent verification.

What card sizes can HGA grade?

HGA Poly comes in 60+ sizes tooled to the card, matched automatically, from standard trading cards to oversized collectibles and booklet formats. If no case exists for your card yet, we make one at no extra fee. Presale slots cover standard size cards; booklets, folds and decks are booked separately.

Can I transfer my pre-launch purchase to someone else?

No. Pre-launch purchases are non-transferable and cannot be transferred to another person or account.

What card categories can I submit?

From sports and TCGs to entertainment and pop culture, including sports, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic, UFC and Disney cards.
